# NASA Blue Marble surface detail

Source: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, “The Blue Marble” (2002),
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/.

Original image:
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a002900/a002915/bluemarble-2048.png

Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
Blue Marble data courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC).

The original 2048×1024 image was transcoded to WebP (quality 86) as
`earth-blue-marble.webp`. The globe applies a subdued blue color grade in its
shader. No SKF imagery or website assets are included.
